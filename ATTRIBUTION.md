# Source provenance

TensorCraft adapts the supplied PromptCraft project. The original source identified Rithik and Aqua0 and referenced NotRithik/edge-city-game-jam-promptcraft as its repository. Those contributors created the pre-existing voxel game, multiplayer, AI action language, agent tooling, and governance features.

The Bittensor Builders House adaptation adds Chutes integration, provider provenance, a standalone demo deployment, updated branding, tests, and presentation materials. Event branding does not imply endorsement by Bittensor, Chutes, HackQuest, or Minecraft/Mojang.
